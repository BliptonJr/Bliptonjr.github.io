Neural Nektar AI-IN-A-CAN

Generative AI & Image Processing

This project explores the use of generative AI techniques to work with images, demonstrating practical applications of neural networks in creative image generation and manipulation.

Project Overview

Objective

The goal of this assignment is to understand and apply generative AI models to image-related tasks. This includes exploring how neural networks can generate, transform, and analyze visual data, while evaluating the technical limitations and creative potential of generative tools.

Implementation

Approach

For the “Using Generative AI to Work with Images” project, I chose to design a lighthearted concept: “AI in a can.” I created a fictional brand and product line named Neural Nektar™. The concept of bottling a high-tech digital intelligence allowed me to experiment with absurd marketing tropes while reflecting how modern consumers view AI as a quick, on-demand solution for every problem.

How I cleaned up the drawings in GIMP:

  • To clean up a hand drawn picture, start by converting the image to grayscale via Colors > Desaturate and then use Colors > Posterize (set to 2) or Colors > Threshold to remove faint pencil marks and isolate the lines.
  • Use the Eraser tool or Paintbrush on a new layer to manually fix specific blemishes.
  • Apply Filters > Enhance > Antialias to smooth jagged edges.
  • Clean up stubborn background noise or scanned artifacts. For stubborn background noise or scanned artifacts, use Filters > Enhance > Despeckle or Filters > Blur > Median Blur followed by Colors > Curves to restore sharp, clean lines.

Product transitions into a technology filled scene (e.g. Server room, computer in gaming room, electronics associated with A.I., minimal studio room with gpu mining rigs in the backround). Subtle zoom-in + rotation keeps motion dynamic. 2–3s (Detail | Acceleration) Top-down macro shot. Pull tab, bottle cap, or surface texture details. Quick push-in + micro rotation transition. 3–4s (Action | Peak 1) Opening moment. Tab pops or cap opens. Cold mist or vapor bursts outward. Mist fills the frame → seamless transition. 4–5s (Energy | Peak 2) Extreme close-up. Bubbles, liquid flow, or carbonation movement. Fast micro motion + subtle zoom pulse. 5–6s (Contrast | Breathing Moment) Product appears in a contrasting environment (ice vs warm light, fresh vs bold mood). Slow rotation + zoom out. 6–8s. Show warning sign can containing the text “Caution: Do not consume. For external algorithmic ambient processing only. 2. Brand Strategy Framework

Brand Mission: "To democratize artificial intelligence by taking it out of the cloud and putting it into aluminum. We believe world-class neural processing should be as portable, refreshing, and instantly accessible as a cold beverage."

Target Audience:

  • Primary: Overworked students, frantic project managers, and late-night coders who need an instant second opinion or an extra set of digital hands.
  • Demographic: Tech-literate Gen Z & Millennials who appreciate satire, sleek product design, and absurd efficiency.

Unique Value Proposition (UVP):

"Instant artificial intelligence with zero latency, zero cloud dependency, and a satisfying crack-of-the-can opening. Just open, pour near your device, and let the ambient liquid logic handle the rest."

3. "Why It's Necessary" (The Pitch)

  • Zero-Cloud Latency: No Wi-Fi? No problem. Having AI in a physical liquid format means local processing power that goes wherever you go—subways, dead zones, or off-grid camping trips.
  • Physical Portability: Cloud servers take up entire warehouses. Canned AI fits in a cupholder, backpack, or vending machine.
  • Tactile Accessibility: Interacting with AI through a screen creates a digital barrier. Liquid AI creates an intuitive, tactile experience—just crack it open when a problem arises.

4. Real-World Use Cases (How It Helps)

  • Late-Night Writing: Crack a can on your desk; ambient micro-bubbles analyze your essay draft and whisper grammar corrections before your deadline.
  • Emergency Coding: Pour half a drop onto a stubborn bug in your script to auto-generate clean documentation and patch leaks.
  • Awkward Social Situations: Open a can in your pocket to stream perfect small-talk prompts directly into your ear.
  • Creative Block: Take a quick whiff of the ozone-flavored vapor for an instant burst of brainstormed product names.

Final Thoughts

In Conclusion

While I worked meticulously with Gemini to help perfect my prompt engineering for the small social media commercial, I didn’t have much faith in a free text/image to video solution such as Pollo.AI. For the most part it worked, and gave me a great little video, but didn’t include much of my prompt. MeshyAI gave me incredible models for the mascot, CHIP, but failed horribly in converting the circuit board logo text, giving me AI slop text instead.

Overall, blending generative AI tools with traditional hand-drawn concepts allowed me to explore both the capabilities and current limitations of emerging design tech. While tools like Pollo.AI and Meshy AI didn't execute every detail perfectly, combining them with prompt engineering and manual editing in GIMP resulted in a cohesive, hilariously professional brand identity for Neural Nektar™. This project highlighted that while AI can rapidly bring wild ideas to life, human oversight and creative direction remain essential to refine the final product
